# Rachel & Jack

The website for Rachel and Jack's wedding, covering their story, the event, and
the gifts guests gave them.

## Language

**Guest**: Anyone using the public site. _Avoid_: Purchaser, gift-giver, donor,
contributor

**Registry Purchase**: A Guest's self-reported claim that they bought a Registry
Item elsewhere. No money moves through this site, and the claim is only as
trustworthy as the Guest's word. _Avoid_: Order, payment, transaction

**Honeymoon Contribution**: An amount of money chosen by a Guest and
successfully paid to the Honeymoon Fund through Stripe. An attempt that was
never paid is not a Honeymoon Contribution. _Avoid_: Donation, honeymoon
purchase
